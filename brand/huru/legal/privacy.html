4. Health and fitness data, and the AI Coach

Your body weight, measurements and training history can reveal things about your health, so we treat them as sensitive data and process them only with your explicit consent.

Your consent. You give it on a consent screen, with an unticked checkbox, before you first use the app. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting that data or your account; this does not affect processing that already happened. Huru GYM is a fitness tool, not a medical service.

What the AI Coach sends. The Coach is a Pro feature that uses OpenAI's API service. Before it first runs, it shows you exactly what leaves Huru GYM and asks for your go-ahead. It sends:

  1. Your plan: routines, exercises, sets, reps, schedule and progression settings
  2. Your training in the period being reviewed: logged sets, targets, effort ratings, durations, records and session notes
  3. Your weigh-ins in that period and your goal weight
  4. The answers you gave the Coach, including any limitations you mentioned
  5. Your units, language and effort scale

It never sends your name, e-mail address, password, photos or payment details. OpenAI processes this data for us as a service provider, under its business terms and data processing addendum: it does not use data sent through its API to train its models, and keeps it for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes it.

Turning it off. Withdraw Coach consent or switch the Coach off in the app, and we delete the Coach data we hold for you on our server straight away.

How we protect it. All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes, and passkeys keep your private key on your own device. Session cookies cannot be read by scripts or sent over plain connections. Only staff who run the service can reach the server. Daily backups are encrypted before they leave it and kept for 30 days, and we are adding full-disk encryption to the server. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Cookies, children, transfers and changes

Children. Huru GYM is not for anyone under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we delete it.

International transfers. AdxFuel LLC is based in the United States, and some providers process data there or elsewhere. When data from the EU, UK or Switzerland leaves those regions, we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, depending on the provider.
