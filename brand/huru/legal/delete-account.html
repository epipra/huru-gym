Delete your Huru GYM account

Huru GYM by AdxFuel LLC · Last updated 12 October 2026

You can delete your account and all its data yourself in the app, at any time; it takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Sign in to Huru GYM on the web or in the app.
  2. Optional: keep a copy first with Settings, Data and backup, Export.
  3. Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account.
  4. Type your profile name and, if your account has one, your password.
  5. Tap Delete my account.

By e-mail

If you cannot sign in, e-mail support@epipra.com from the address on your account, or tell us your profile name. We confirm it is your account and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, sign-in details (passkeys, password, paired devices) and consent records
  • Your plans, workouts, records, body weight, measurements, photos and videos
  • Your AI Coach history
  • Your Huru GYM Pro subscription, which is cancelled at once, with no refund for the rest of the current period

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: copies age out within 60 days of deletion.
  • Payment and invoice records: kept by us and Stripe as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually up to 7 years.
  • Security log: an entry that the account was deleted, kept for 90 days.
